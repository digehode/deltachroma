It's a small update in the sense there is still not a single feature
built, but a big one in the sense that everything is in place ready to
begin.

I'm not really a javascript/typescript programmer, but that's what I'm going with for a few reasons:

1. I think it makes sense to do this in the browser rather than on the server. It can be on a static site (this one) and not need to do heavy processing aynchronously or within reasonable time for a request response.
2. I want to learn a little more about modern javascript/typescript. All my other development work is with Python, often with Django. With HTMX, I can get away with barely any custom front-end logic. But I do need it sometimes and this is a chance to play, upon a clean slate, with modern setups for this kind of thing.
3. There are some great libraries for dealing with colour that I can use and get to know.
4. It's in the right context. By that, I mean the whole point of DeltaChroma is to create palettes for charts, mostly on the web. So working in the right context means less translating back and forth.

I also picked Astro for generating the pages. I used mkdocs for a long time and still do for various things, but with material for mkdocs coming to an end, it seemed time to look at the options. Another project (internal) uses Quarto for documentation. This one uses Astro. The next may use something else.  I'm hoping one of them jumps out at me as the one that fits my way of working.
